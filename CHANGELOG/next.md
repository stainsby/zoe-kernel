**Adopter notes.**

1. **A work session no longer opens a management session by its own act.** The *Hand off*
   paragraph of `kernel/skills/zoe-run/SKILL.md` used to say that where an assessment is due,
   the results go to assess, "which makes this a management session" — and, otherwise, that
   the session ends with its records written and the next management session assesses them.
   The first branch let a work session turn itself into a management session without regard
   to who the index says may open one. It now says: the results go to assess in this session
   if it is already a management session, otherwise in the next one, which opens only as your
   index says; a work session does not open one by its own act. The second branch is
   unchanged in substance. Compare that paragraph first. The rule itself was already the
   instructions' (*The cycle*: assess runs only in a management session; your index lists when
   one may occur) — the hand-off now defers to it instead of contradicting it.
   **What to do:** nothing, unless one of your own skills cites `zoe-run`'s hand-off — re-read
   those against the new wording.
